# Weeknight Pasta

A quick and delicious pasta dish perfect for busy weeknights.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb pasta (penne or spaghetti)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Grated Parmesan cheese
- Fresh basil for garnish (optional)

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
3. Add minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour in crushed tomatoes, dried basil, and oregano. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Combine cooked pasta with the sauce and toss to coat.
7. Serve hot with grated Parmesan cheese and fresh basil if desired.

Serves 4. Preparation time: 20 minutes.
